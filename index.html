text.compare

Compare two texts and see exactly what changed. Everything happens in your browser: your text is never uploaded, and this site has no ads, analytics or cookies. Links you copy carry the text inside the link itself.

Tips

  • Drop two files at once to compare them straight away.
  • Both sides are editable. Use the arrows between them to copy a block across.
  • Options can ignore case, whitespace and blank lines.
